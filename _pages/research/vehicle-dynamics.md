## Research Overview

My research investigates integrated powertrain and vehicle-level
dynamics control, with a focus on tire forces, vehicle stability,
and coordinated motion control.

## Key Research Topics

### Tire Force Modeling

Physics-based modeling of tire forces and their relationship
to vehicle motion and road conditions.

### Integrated Vehicle Control

Coordinating powertrain actuation and vehicle dynamics control
to improve stability and handling performance.
